# Clipboard Tweaks

A modern clipboard manager that replaces the Windows Win+V panel with a Fluent 2 window: search your clipboard history, keep pinned items organized in categories with tags, and paste with one click.

> **Status: in development.** No version has been released yet. This page describes the planned first release, and screenshots will be added with it.

## Features

- **Replaces Win+V**: Win+V opens Clipboard Tweaks instead of the built-in panel. Turn the mod off in Windhawk and the built-in panel comes back.
- **History, Pinned and Templates**: three tabs keep recent items, the items you want to keep, and reusable text apart.
- **Search**: click the magnifier in the title bar or press Ctrl+F to filter items as you type, including by `#tag`.
- **Pinned items that stay**: organize pinned items into categories with an emoji and a color, and label them with tags. Pinned items are only ever removed by you: restarts, mod updates and history limits never delete them.
- **Item details**: open an item to see its full content and act on it from the command bar.
- **Clear what you see**: **Clear** removes the items in the current view only, such as the history or a single category. Templates are removed one by one.
- **Window that fits your workflow**: resize it from any edge, keep it on top with the pin button, and reopen it from the tray icon. Its size and position are remembered, and can be fixed in the settings.
- **Shortcut help**: hover the **i** icon in the footer to see every keyboard shortcut.
- **Move to another PC**: export pinned items, categories, tags, templates and settings to a password-protected file, then merge or replace them on the other computer.

## How to use

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| Win+V | Open Clipboard Tweaks |
| Ctrl+F | Search |

## Data and privacy

Pinned items and templates are stored encrypted with your Windows account, so other users of the computer can't read them. Export files are protected with a password you choose.

## Compatibility

- Requires clipboard history to be turned on: **Settings › System › Clipboard › Clipboard history**.
- Windhawk 1.7.3 or newer.
- Tested Windows versions will be listed with the first release.
